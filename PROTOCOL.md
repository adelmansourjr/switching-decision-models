# Frozen simulation protocol

Adel Mansour · 11 November 2026

## Scope and hypotheses

This first study concerns two simulated periodic threshold relays. It isolates timing, duty cycle and decision costs before considering a physical apparatus or strategic opponents. Time is normalized to one scoring window. Each independent scenario is one synthetic configuration and set of latent phase and sensor draws. The two confirmation mechanisms are fixed strata, not a random sample of all switching mechanisms.

**Mechanism.** Reset phase from half a cycle apart to coincident with all other scenario variables held fixed. Require the lower 97.5% marginal paired interval for the joint-high time gain to exceed 0.10. Require both individual-channel change intervals to lie strictly inside ±0.005 as evidence of practical equivalence. Report the fraction of scenarios in which the action paying the joint-high fraction beats a fixed payoff of the product of the two duty fractions at synchrony and loses at half-cycle separation. This is a separate contrast from the active sensing experiment.

**Decision.** At the unknown natural phase, choose whether to buy a noisy phase measurement, then whether to pay a scenario-specific cost to shift the phase. Measure net payoff against never shifting. Require both lower 97.5% marginal paired endpoints for targeted sensing versus the two primary controls to exceed 0.002 of normalized payoff. The interval convention gives nominal 95% simultaneous coverage for the two decision comparisons. For the three mechanistic conditions, success requires all three conditions. Marginal coverage and paired effect estimates will be reported, without treating different outcomes as a single confidence interval.

## World, information and evaluation

Channel 1 has integer frequency \(N\), sampled from 24 to 90 cycles per scoring window. Channel 2 has phase \(Nt+\phi+\Delta t+\beta w(t)\) with average drift \(\Delta\) uniform on [−.45,.45] cycles and \(\beta\) uniform on [−.16,.16]. Independent duty fractions are uniform on [.34,.66]. Natural phase is uniform on [0,1). Linear and sinusoidal timing are used in fitting and development. Confirmation uses a smooth rate sweep with \(w(t)=4t(1-t)\), and a piecewise-linear phase path with \(w(t)=1-|2t-1|\) giving a discrete change in rate. These mechanisms are distinct functional forms. They share ranges and other ingredients with the development simulator. Both have zero perturbation at the window endpoints. In the phase-reset experiment only, drift is divided by four to test near nominal synchronization.

Before deciding whether to measure, the policy sees the duty fractions, average drift, phase-shift option and its known cost, and a coarse phase reading. The reading is the latent phase plus independent wrapped Gaussian error of standard deviation .14 cycles. A second reading is available only if purchased, with independent standard deviation .026 cycles. The policy has no access to the within-window shape, amplitude, true phase or future outcomes. Sensor fee is 0.008 of normalized payoff. The phase shift is uniform on [.045,.34] cycles and costs uniform on [.025,.18]. In the simulation shifts are instantaneous and change only phase. For the scoring function both scenarios for a paid shift use the same hidden parameters. A full-information diagnostic takes the better arm without paying for a sensor, so its gross value is headroom.

Find all threshold event times by bisection in a monotone phase path and integrate the step functions over exact-duration intervals. Confirm numerical convergence by separately increasing bisection precision on representative cases and independently verifying ideal phase limits. Retain event errors even when they are negligible. Data are generated on CPU with no devices or human subjects.

## Policies and budget

The physics model integrates the unequal-duty circular overlap along a constant relative rate. It uses the known observation-noise model and a uniform phase prior. It discretizes the phase circle into 96 points and integrates along 28 equally spaced time midpoints. For each context it computes expected gains before and after a possible measurement and the preposterior expected value of sampling. Per mechanism, select positive-net measurements in priority order up to 35% of contexts. This is a batch-budget experiment. The allocation can use the set of coarse contexts in the batch and cannot use fine readings until the corresponding decisions are selected.

Both primary controls receive the exact same measurement count and fee, the same coarse information, and the same calibrated sensor likelihood. The margin policy uses the same physics gain model and updates as the proposed policy, but allocates measurements to the smallest absolute blind decision margin. The learned-value policy fits a regularized Fourier regression on the outcome labels of both actions using 2,100 independently simulated training contexts and privileged true phase in training only. At evaluation it applies the same observation, budget and preposterior calculation to its estimated gains. The comparison controls for budget and separates the allocation rule from the transfer value of the physics model. Also report random equal-budget, no-measurement and always-measure policies. The always-measure diagnostic uses a larger budget.

## Split, uncertainty and reproducibility

Use independent seeded training and development data. Development is used to verify behavior and diagnose implementation. Freeze the specification, source, fit and development summary before drawing the confirmation seed from OS randomness. Generate 1,000 independent configurations per withheld mechanism. Reuse each scenario for all paired policies and phase conditions. Average the two strata equally. Compute 10,000 paired bootstrap draws, resampling configurations within strata. Save the bank, scenario-level scores, seed receipt and aggregated results. Replay must verify these exactly on the same numerical platform. This is a local chronological freeze. Custody is internal and the withheld family implementations are known to the experimenter. The policy is evaluated on new draws and family structures outside fitting and development.

The test can fail through insufficient decision gain, failed equivalence, inability to predict the joint effect, or numerical instability. An advantage on the phase-reset experiment alone supports the relay mechanism under the simulator's assumptions. A programmable physical relay, actual contact mechanics, and strategic transfer each require separate evidence.
